Floods start in many ways: heavy rain, storm surge, a river over its banks, a dead sump pump, a sewer backup or a major plumbing failure, and all of them call for fast professional help. Expect gray water (Category 2) or black water (Category 3) in almost any flood, and with it bacteria, contamination and potentially hazardous substances that threaten your health. Day or night, 24/7, Coon Rapids Restoration sends flood cleanup crews throughout Coon Rapids to remove floodwater, sanitize the affected rooms and safely restore your home or building.

Cleanup opens with rapid extraction: truck-mounted units and submersible pumps that pull thousands of gallons an hour out of the building. Next our technicians, who hold IICRC certification, judge the level of contamination and choose a sanitizing plan that covers HEPA air filtration, antimicrobial application and disinfecting each surface that got wet. Anything unsalvageable comes out and is hauled off correctly, including wet insulation, drywall that took in contaminated water and waterlogged carpet padding.

After extraction and sanitizing, we set up commercial drying equipment, including LGR (low-grain refrigerant) dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers, to dry the structure of your property thoroughly. We check moisture levels every day with calibrated meters and thermal imaging so the drying is complete and mold does not get a start. Every stage of the job is recorded for your claim: photographs, detailed moisture maps, logs of the equipment used and the scope-of-work reports that insurance adjusters require.

Why Flood Cleanup Is Not a DIY Job

Flood cleanup is far more than pumping out standing water. The water brings sewage, chemicals, bacteria and more with it, and all of that is a serious hazard to health. A trained cleanup team knows how to set containment, wear PPE (personal protective equipment) correctly and sanitize by protocol, which protects your household from hepatitis, E. coli and other diseases carried in water.

Health is not the only issue: when cleanup is done wrong, moisture stays hidden and heavy mold can follow in a matter of days. Our technicians rely on psychrometric calculations, meter readings and thermal imaging to verify that each affected spot, from the framing to the subfloor to the inside of the walls, meets accepted drying standards. Nothing else reliably heads off secondary damage, and that damage can run thousands more to remediate, so we dry by the numbers.

Warning Signs for Flood Cleanup

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Living areas flooded by contaminated water from a backed-up drain or sewer
  • Mud, silt or debris left on floors once the water goes down
  • Carpet that squishes underfoot or drywall that feels soft and wet
  • Cracked foundation walls, low doorways or window wells letting water inside
  • Standing water anywhere on your property, whatever the source
  • Storm damage that lets rain in through the roof or walls
  • Appliances such as a water heater, washer or dishwasher spilling water across the floor

How to Prevent Flood Cleanup Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • A battery-powered backup sump pump keeps water moving if the electricity fails in a storm
  • Cracks in the foundation should be sealed and the basement walls given a waterproof coating
  • Store valuables and important papers off the basement floor in waterproof containers
  • Have a backwater valve fitted to the sewer line so sewage cannot flow back inside
  • Consider flood insurance even if you are not in a designated flood zone
  • Wherever flooding is likely, get appliances and electrical equipment up above floor level
