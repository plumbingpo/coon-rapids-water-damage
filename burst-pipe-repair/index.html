A burst pipe is one of the most common water damage events a property can face, and potentially one of the most destructive. One broken line can put out 4 to 8 gallons every minute, so 240 to 480 gallons an hour may be running through floors, ceilings and walls. The trigger may be a hard freeze, corroded or aging lines, high pressure or physical damage, but the need is the same: act immediately so water does less harm to the structure, the floors, your possessions and the building's systems.

Day or night, Coon Rapids Restoration responds to burst pipe emergencies across Coon Rapids and the nearby communities. Technicians on our team assist with finding and closing the water supply, while licensed plumbers we coordinate with handle a proper plumbing repair. With the source under control, our crew goes straight to extraction, using truck-mounted machines and portable extractors to clear standing water from each affected area quickly.

Structural drying comes next and covers the wet drywall, the framing, the insulation, the subfloor and the finished flooring above it. Since water runs downhill through ceiling spaces, floor assemblies and wall cavities, a burst pipe frequently affects several rooms on more than one level. Hidden moisture leads to mold and a deteriorating structure, so we use moisture detection equipment and thermal imaging to map the water's path and make sure all affected areas get dried.

What a Professional Brings to Burst Pipe Repair

The damage from a burst pipe tends to reach well past the wet areas in plain view. Materials get saturated in spots that can stay undiscovered for days or weeks, because the water runs along conduits, joists, floor assemblies and the insides of walls. At that point mold can already be growing out of sight, and the structure may have lost a significant amount of strength. With moisture meters and thermal cameras, trained technicians follow the water's path and pinpoint every affected area right away.

The machines we use for extraction and drying are in another class from anything sold for home use. Compared with a shop vacuum, truck-mounted extraction is hundreds of times faster, and compared with a residential dehumidifier, a commercial one removes 10 to 20 times as much moisture. That speed and power are essential when you are racing the 24 to 48 hour window before mold growth begins.

When to Call for Burst Pipe Repair in Coon Rapids

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Water charges that jump for no clear reason, a sign of a concealed leak
  • Unexplained standing water along baseboards or in the middle of a room
  • Faucets and showers across the building suddenly running weak
  • A stained, soggy or bowed section of drywall overhead or beside you
  • Stale or earthy smells coming from a cabinet or wall cavity
  • Hearing water move through the house while nothing is turned on
  • Brownish water at the faucet, which can mean corroded pipes

Water rarely stays where the pipe broke. It runs downhill inside walls, under floors and across ceilings. Conduits for wiring and the gaps between joists carry it even farther. Tracing the migration with moisture meters and thermal imaging lets us locate all of the damage.

How to Prevent Burst Pipe Repair Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Going away in cold months? Keep the thermostat running to protect the plumbing from freezing
  • Check water pressure with a gauge, since anything over 80 PSI puts stress on fittings and pipes
  • Every few years, ask a licensed plumber to look over the system for corrosion and worn parts
  • Everyone in the house should be able to locate and turn the main water valve in a hurry
  • During freezing weather, keep faucets dripping and cabinet doors open so heat can circulate
  • Aging galvanized or polybutylene plumbing should be swapped out ahead of an unexpected failure
