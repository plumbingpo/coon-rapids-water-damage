Drying a home is only half the job. After readings return to normal, the bare subfloor, cut-out drywall and missing floors still need attention. Rebuilding all of that is what water damage repair means. Our team in Coon Rapids replaces what could not be saved and matches it to what was there before, so the room looks like a room again and not a job site.

No two repair jobs are the same size. On a small job it may be a few feet of drywall, new baseboard and paint. On a large one it can mean insulation, full sheets of drywall, subfloor, hardwood or laminate, cabinet boxes, trim and doors. We replace materials like for like where possible, and we tell you plainly when an exact match is not available so you can choose.

We do not close anything in while it is still damp. Before any new material goes on, we confirm with a moisture meter that framing and subfloor are at normal levels. Cover wood that is still damp, and mold grows unseen behind the new paint. Before we begin, a schedule and scope in writing tell you which items are being rebuilt and the sequence.

How to Tell You Need Water Damage Repair

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Wood floor boards stayed raised, cupped or crowned even once the moisture was gone.
  • Paint is bubbling, peeling or stained brown where water ran down the wall.
  • Cabinet bases or side panels are swollen, soft or peeling at the bottom.
  • The edges of your laminate or vinyl planks have puffed up, curled or pulled apart.
  • Since the water damage, certain doors drag on the jamb or miss the latch.
  • Walking over the formerly wet area, you feel soft spots in the floor.
  • Wall cavities remain open where drywall was removed for drying.

The Benefits of Professional Water Damage Repair

Rebuilding after a water loss differs from a normal remodel. The person rebuilding needs to know what the water did, where it went and whether the structure behind the wall is truly dry. We build from the moisture records made during drying, which tell us the studs that got wet, the subfloor that was treated and the spots needing a second look. That history keeps problems from being covered over with new drywall and fresh paint.

The second skill is matching. You will see a visible patch every single day. We blend wall texture, feather paint to natural break points and source flooring that lines up with the existing boards where it can be found. When a match is not possible, we lay out options, such as replacing flooring to a doorway. We also handle permits where the work requires them and keep your insurer updated.

How to Prevent Water Damage Repair Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Rebuilt bathroom walls last longer when the exhaust fan runs through and after each shower.
  • Hold off on heavy traffic and wet cleaning until new adhesives, caulk and paint have cured.
  • Ask for final moisture readings before any contractor closes up a wall or floor.
  • Fix the source first, and only then patch or paint a water stain.
  • Keep leftover flooring, tile and paint details so future repairs can be matched.
  • Fresh caulk every year around showers, tubs and sinks stops water reaching the wall cavity.
